November in the groves

The season of liquid gold

 

Right now, all over Corfu, nets are being spread beneath the olive trees. Families gather the harvest by hand, just as their grandparents did, and the first green-gold oil of the year flows from the presses.

Many of the island’s trees were planted in Venetian times, and some have been bearing fruit for hundreds of years. So when you sit in their shade next spring, remember: they were here long before us, and they’ll still be here when you come back.

“Some places you visit.
Others you come home to.”

Beyond the beach

History just around the corner

 

Take a walk south from Kavos and the noise soon gives way to birdsong. A path through the woods leads you to the ruins of the Panagia Arkoudilla monastery and on to the white cliffs of Cape Asprokavos, the southern tip of the island. Just a short drive north, Lefkimmi is waiting for you with its river, fishing boats and bell towers. And Corfu Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, makes a lovely day trip.
